Scope surprise

Ingredient units do not add up yet

Complexity mediumRisk of breaking low
Recipe ARecipe BMerged
200 g rice1 cup rice?

Recipes store units as free text. Merging a week's list needs a unit table, which is more than the plan screen itself.

Default: merge only exact unit matches, list the rest twice. Needs your answer.

Behaviour change

The shopping list becomes week-based

Complexity lowRisk of breaking medium

Today the list belongs to one recipe. If the plan owns it, opening a recipe's list from its page has to change or go.

Default: keep both; the recipe page shows its own list, the plan shows the merged one.

Decisions

  1. Units. A: exact matches only, rest listed twice (default). B: a small unit table now. C: no merging, one list per day.
  2. Recipe list. Keep per-recipe lists (default), or replace with the plan's.
  3. Plan length. Seven days (default), or five.

Answer by number: "1 B, 2 ok, 3 ok".
